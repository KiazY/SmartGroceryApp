Privacy Policy — Smart Grocery List

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Smart Grocery List ("the App") is designed so that all of your data stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what the App does and does not do with your information.

Summary: we do not collect your data

The App does not have a backend server, does not require an account, and does not send your grocery list, your voice, or any personal data to us or to any third party. All processing happens locally on your phone.

Speech-to-text (dictation)

If you use the microphone button to dictate a grocery request, the App uses your device's built-in on-device speech recognition. Your voice audio is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded anywhere — not to us, not to Apple, not to Google, not to any other server.

On-device AI (grocery list parsing)

The App uses a local, on-device language model (Llama 3.2 1B Instruct) to turn your typed or dictated text into a structured grocery list. This model runs entirely on your device. The text you type or dictate is never sent to any server for this processing.

On first launch, the App downloads the model file itself (not your data) from a public model repository so it can run locally. This is a one-time download of the AI model, not a transmission of your information.

Data storage

Your grocery list is stored only in the App's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted off your device, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not use any third-party service that processes your personal data.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that requires data collection), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
